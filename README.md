# Itihasa_Hackwave3.0
**What if the monuments we walk past every day could tell us their stories?**
Many children and adults pass India’s forts, temples, sculptures, and historic sites without knowing the people, struggles, achievements, and forgotten stories behind them. These places are not just buildings. They are living reminders of who we are and where we came from.
Itihasa is different from ordinary AI agents because it teaches history through memorable storytelling, emotion, context, and curiosity. It transforms overlooked monuments into meaningful experiences that people can understand, remember, and carry with them.
Because history is not just something to learn.It is something to discover, remember, value, and pass forward.

Itihasa - Discover the forgotten,Remember the story.
